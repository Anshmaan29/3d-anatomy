# Quality check — 28 October 2026

## Practice improvements

Practice now displays the asked structure by itself by default and colours it gold in dark mode or blue in light mode. A student can show the active systems as translucent context without revealing the answer. Camera fitting accounts for the viewer aspect ratio, including the smaller practice area on phones. Section and exploded views are disabled during an identification question so they cannot cut or displace the target. The atlas retains its normal appearance after practice.

Options are deduplicated by displayed name and prioritise the same system, region and source group. Sessions with fewer than four distinct visible names now explain why no question is available. A question can be skipped without changing the score, and exploring an answer selects the same structure in the atlas. On narrow screens, the question sheet and highlighted model share the screen; selecting a system closes the mobile drawer. Practice sharing is disabled until shareable questions are supported, avoiding an unrelated link. Saved annotation focus now targets its attached point, including in exploded view.

## Verification and limits

- TypeScript/Vite build and all 60 automated tests passed, including practice generation against both catalogues and camera-interaction regression checks.
- In-browser checks covered urinary and nervous practice, context, answer/reveal/scoring, skip, light/dark colours, source-based revision and reveal, saved workspace, search, selection, isolate/restore and zoom controls. Phone and iPad-sized browser layouts were inspected. A 3D note was placed and saved; its focus marker was confirmed at the centre of the viewer, then the temporary note was deleted. A temporary saved view, bookmark, screenshot and collection export were checked and removed. An annotation-only collection can now be exported, and its downloaded JSON was verified to contain the note.
- This is not exhaustive verification of every feature or device. Real iPad and Android touch/performance, slow-network and accessibility testing, all questions, and qualified medical review remain outstanding. Catalogue labels and source muscle descriptions are not independently reviewed; the source catalogue includes spelling issues that need expert review.

Licence/provenance files are present in the bundle, while the owner requested removal of the credits interface.
